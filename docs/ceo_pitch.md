# Compass — Why Invest (CEO-facing)

Compass turns "we think this will help" into "here's what actually worked for
companies like yours" — and it does it automatically. When a COO describes an
operational problem, Compass finds real-world evidence of similar organizations
that solved it, ranks the approaches by what measurably worked, and produces a
defensible decision brief with the confidence, comparables, risks, and
implementation plan attached. That's the difference between betting on a
vendor's pitch and betting on proof.

What makes it worth paying for is that this evidence engine is **self-improving**.
A budget-controlled AI agent runs around the clock — no staff required —
deepening evidence on existing cases and hunting down new ones only where your
decisions have gaps. The agent does not collect data because it exists; it
collects because a specific decision needs it. Every dollar it spends is capped
and tied to closing a measurable gap, and it tracks cost per useful record and
whether the briefs actually got better — so the quality of your decisions
compounds over time.

Compass is built as three connected systems:

- **Evidence Operations** — continuously improving the implementation
  intelligence (inspect gaps → plan targeted campaigns → discover/fetch/extract
  → quality-gated publish → benchmark → learn).
- **Decision Engine** — turning that intelligence into defensible
  recommendations (canonical organization/industry taxonomy, context-aware
  retrieval, evidence tiers, confidence).
- **Implementation System** — helping teams execute and measure the result
  (implementation plans, workspaces, success metrics).

You are not buying a report. You are buying decision-making infrastructure that
gets more accurate, more relevant, and more cost-efficient the longer it runs —
and it is ready to extend into Compass Pro and new decision domains without
rebuilding the foundation.
